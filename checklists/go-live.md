Check a box only when the action is performed; record result `pass`, `fail`, `hold` or `n/a` with evidence and independent reviewer/date. `n/a` needs approved scope rationale. Missing or stale evidence/approval is a **hold**; a failed required criterion is a **block**. **Neither permits launch or expansion.** An eligible, independently approved, scope-matched, unexpired exception must be explicit and tested; it does not erase failed evidence. During rollout, hold expansion or contain/roll back per the approved risk/runbook when a criterion fails.

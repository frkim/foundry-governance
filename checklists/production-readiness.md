Check boxes for performed reviews, not presumed success. Every item needs executed evidence for the release candidate, independent reviewer/date and status `pass`, `fail`, `hold` or `n/a`. `n/a` requires approved scope rationale. Missing, stale or unverifiable evidence is a **hold**; a failed required criterion is a **block**. Both stop release. Any eligible deviation needs a separately approved, scope-matched, unexpired exception; retain the underlying failed finding.

## How to use the catalog

GOV-01 through GOV-12 are the canonical identifiers throughout this repository. A control is a desired organizational outcome, **not** a claim that Foundry or Azure Policy implements it. Populate one control record per applicable workload/environment with `control_id`, `agent_id`, `deployment_id`, `version`, owner, implementation, evidence URI, evidence timestamp, reviewer, review date, decision, exception ID and next review date. Shared controls may reference centrally held evidence, but the workload owner must establish applicability.

The cadences below are proposed defaults. Ratify them, shorten them for high-risk systems, and review after material changes regardless of cadence. Evidence must match the released version and actual deployment; screenshots alone do not demonstrate continuing effectiveness. Mark a control `pass`, `fail`, `hold` or `n/a`; `n/a` needs a written scope rationale and independent reviewer. A finding without an owner and due date is not a managed finding.

### Review cadence precedence

Use the stricter applicable control, domain or high-risk cadence after organizational ratification; a quarterly governance meeting does not defer operational checks. The following distinctions resolve the catalog's default schedules:

| Activity | Proposed minimum | Authoritative detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory/deployment reconciliation | Weekly, plus release and material dependency/owner changes | [Inventory policy](../../docs/25-ai-inventory/README.md#policy) |
| Exception expiry checks | Weekly planning review; validate at each affected release and act at the actual expiry, not the next scheduled review | [Maintenance cadence](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#review-cadence-and-ownership), [exception lifecycle](../policies/README.md#exceptions) |
| Exception compensation/remediation review | Monthly, more often for high risk; immediately on compensation failure | [Exception monitoring](../policies/README.md#exceptions) |
| Fleet governance review | Quarterly, without replacing the checks above | [Operating model](../../docs/01-governance-model/README.md#platform-onboarding-and-review) |

## Evidence quality and decision rules

- Evidence has a named producer and independent reviewer, timestamp, environment, artifact/configuration version, collection method and access-controlled URI. Record a checksum for exported evidence bundles where feasible.
- Retain approvals and test records according to the organization's approved evidence schedule, not indefinitely by default. Do not place credentials, raw personal data or production conversation bodies in this repository.
- `fail` on a release-required control is a **block**. Missing or unverifiable evidence is a **hold**, which also prevents release until resolved. An approved, eligible exception changes the disposition only for its precise scope and validity period; it does not make a failed technical test pass.
- Map risk findings to one or more stable GOV IDs. Add implementation-specific subchecks locally without renumbering the canonical catalog.
